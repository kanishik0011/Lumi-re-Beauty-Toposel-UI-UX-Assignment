# Lumière Beauty — portfolio case study draft

## 1. Overview

Lumière Beauty is a fictional premium beauty e-commerce homepage designed and implemented as a Toposel UI/UX assignment.

## 2. Objective

Create a high-converting shopping experience that feels editorial, trustworthy, and calm rather than like a generic marketplace template.

## 3. Target audience

Beauty shoppers who value considered curation, refined presentation, and an easy path from inspiration to purchase.

## 4. Visual direction

Warm ivory, blush, muted rose, and charcoal create a feminine-but-grounded palette. Playfair Display creates editorial contrast, while DM Sans keeps commerce information legible. Original product still lifes keep the visual language coherent across hero, category, and product surfaces.

## 5. Wireframe explanation

The low-fidelity desktop and mobile exports establish the sequence: header → hero → categories → products → promotion → footer. The mobile wireframe prioritizes a compact header, stacked hero, and two-column discovery surfaces.

## 6. Design system

The system uses an 8px spacing rhythm, 1px dividers, restrained radii, and two button treatments. Muted rose is reserved for emphasis, status, and links so CTAs remain visually clear.

## 7. Desktop/mobile designs

The coded homepage is the high-fidelity reference. Desktop uses large negative space and a four-column rhythm; mobile deliberately crops imagery and reflows content instead of simply scaling it down.

## 8. UX decisions

Search, filters, category navigation, add-to-bag, wishlist, and newsletter validation are functional demo interactions. Product claims and benefit copy remain illustrative and non-medical.

## 9. Accessibility

Landmarks, labels, touch-friendly buttons, readable contrast, reduced motion support, and non-color-only states are included in the prototype.

## 10. Final outcome

The result is a responsive, art-directed storefront with a clear product discovery path and editable design artifacts ready for Figma import.
