# Lumière Beauty V2 — V1 Design Audit

## Context

V1 already established a warm editorial beauty storefront with a sticky header,
category navigation, product filtering, wishlist and cart feedback, newsletter
validation, and a complete footer. V2 keeps that foundation while giving the
experience a more intentional luxury-retail rhythm.

## Visual opportunities

1. The current hero relies on a simple split layout; V2 needs a stronger
   editorial focal point with a compact utility rail and a more deliberate
   headline-to-image relationship.
2. The V1 palette is soft but the hierarchy between blush, ivory, and rose can
   flatten at section boundaries. V2 introduces semantic warm-neutral surfaces
   and one controlled clay accent.
3. Product cards use repeated square crops and similar visual weight. V2 uses a
   taller art-directed media ratio, stronger product metadata, and a clearer
   price/action baseline.
4. Category tiles are functional but visually equal. V2 gives the ritual
   categories a more magazine-like sequence with numbered labels and varied
   composition.
5. Promotional content sits as a single large block. V2 treats it as an
   editorial story panel with a dark contrast surface and an intentional
   secondary CTA.
6. Small labels and descriptions in V1 are easy to lose on low-contrast
   surfaces. V2 raises minimum text sizes and contrast while preserving the
   quiet tone.

## UX opportunities

1. Keep “All” as the default collection state so the first product grid
   communicates breadth immediately.
2. Make search, filters, wishlist, add-to-bag feedback, and newsletter status
   visibly stateful with accessible labels and live feedback.
3. Keep the primary shopping CTA visible in the hero and repeat a contextual
   collection CTA after category discovery.
4. Use consistent product-card action placement so comparison scanning is
   faster across the grid.
5. Keep the sticky navigation compact and expose the mobile menu as a clear
   modal-like panel with predictable dismissal.
6. Preserve keyboard focus indicators and reduced-motion behavior as first-class
   interactions rather than decorative additions.

## Mobile opportunities

1. Reduce the hero crop height and move the copy below the image so the first
   viewport contains the product story and CTA without feeling crowded.
2. Keep category and product grids at two columns with larger tap targets and
   stable card heights.
3. Make the mobile search field expand beneath the header and keep the cart
   count visible while the menu is open.
4. Stack newsletter fields with a full-width submit button and readable
   validation feedback.

## V2 direction

“Modern beauty, elevated by thoughtful rituals.” The redesign uses a warm
  porcelain base, dark editorial panels, a restrained clay accent, serif display
  typography, and stronger composition rather than decorative effects.
